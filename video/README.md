# WickSight — demo videos (Remotion)

Two cuts, both 1920×1080, 30 fps, with captions:

- **60 s key-features cut** (`WickSight1Min`) — English voiceover and a
  music bed at 25% (ducked under the VO). The v4 timeline is concept 0–8 s,
  Telegram alert 8–14.6 s, glance 14.6–18.6 s, voice alert 18.6–22.6 s,
  multi-position 22.6–25 s, close half **25–41 s**, receipt 41–46.4 s,
  Even Hub simulator 46.4–49.9 s, safety/logo/links 49.9–60 s. The v4 voice
  follows `vo-script-v4.md` exactly and the captions follow the voiced words.
  The release asset `wicksight-final.mp4` is still the earlier cut until the
  owner approves; the v4 review render is `wicksight-60s-v4.mp4` (local only).
- **2:59 full cut** (`WickSight`) — cold open + hook + reveal + seven proof
  beats + safety + CTA, silent. The release asset `wicksight-full.mp4` is the
  original 2:55 full cut; `wicksight-final-v2.mp4` is the 2:59 cold-open full cut.

The HUD, type and proof beats are generated by Remotion from the real 23 Nov
2026 Binance Futures **testnet** captures in `public/`; there is no live
capture. The 0–8 s concept open and the close-half footage at 25–41 s are
AI-generated, labelled as such.

## Honesty rules

- The 0–8 s cold open is **AI-generated concept footage**, not live or
  captured footage. It is labelled `CONCEPT VISUALIZATION · AI-GENERATED ·
  NOT LIVE FOOTAGE` on screen. The real 576×288 WickSight HUD is composited
  over it in Remotion; the AI shot contains no HUD text.
- The close-half beat (25–41 s) is also a **concept visualization**: the
  whisper and temple-tap footage comes from `public/gemini-concept.mp4`
  (AI-generated), intercut with the real HUD as a lens. The gesture rail
  (`voice: close half`, `swipe on temple → Yes`, `tap to confirm`) describes
  the interaction; the footage is not a screen recording of the glasses. Every
  AI shot carries the `CONCEPT VISUALIZATION · AI-GENERATED · NOT LIVE
  FOOTAGE` label, and the lens panel says `HUD REPLAY · TESTNET`.
- The Telegram beat is a **mock alert**, not a live Telegram capture. The
  phone UI is a mock; the message text is the real `etaMessage` format from
  `server/telegram.ts` with the captured BTC values. It is labelled
  `MOCK ALERT · REAL TELEGRAM MESSAGE FORMAT`.
- The multi-position beat and the Even Hub screen use the browser simulator's
  data, labelled `MULTI · SIMULATOR` and `SIMULATOR · NO NETWORK · NO ORDERS`.
- The rest of the cut is the 23 Nov testnet reconstruction, labelled
  `REPLAY · RECORDED 23 NOV 2026 · BINANCE FUTURES TESTNET` and
  `REPLAY · NO LIVE MARKET DATA` on screen.
- The proof section is **not live footage**. It is a reconstruction from the
  23 Nov 2026 testnet captures, labelled `REPLAY`, `TESTNET PROOF · REPLAY` and
  `NO LIVE MARKET DATA` on screen.
- The captured values are used throughout: entry ≈ 61,492.9, mark 61,486.88,
  liq 58,684.4, distance 4.6%, position 0.01 BTCUSDT long at 20×, half-fill at
  61,502.9, 0.005 left.
- The v4 voice beat uses `alert 61,500` and sets an armed alert; it does not
  claim the captured 61,486.88 hit. The app's listening screen still shows its
  generic `alert 62,000` prompt; the command itself is a different price. The
  parser supports any price.
- The ETA uses a clean 700 USDT/min tape toward the captured liq line, so the
  HUD reads `LIQ IN ~4m at this pace · buffer 4.6%`. The live app fits the last
  three minutes of real 1 s ticks and hides the line when the tape is flat,
  noisy or moving away.
- The receipt uses the recorded pre-close position and the exchange's
  isolated-margin formula to derive the post-close liq (`58,684 → 55,591`,
  buffer `4.6% → 9.6%`). The live app takes both values from the Binance
  position snapshots before and after the order, not from this formula.
- The reveal shows the captured −0.06 USDT exactly; the proof HUD ticks around
  it. Both are replays, not a live feed.
- `v1.0.0` replaces the old `wicksight-final.mp4` asset with the earlier 60 s
  cut so the already-submitted link keeps working. The v4 review render is
  local only; no release asset was uploaded or changed for v4.

Measured clips: 8.00 s, 6.48 s, 8.00 s, 3.28 s, 14.40 s, 5.28 s, 3.44 s,
10.00 s. Nothing is sped up or cut with `atempo`. Four lines run longer than
the script window:

- line 2: 6.48 s in a 6 s window, **+0.48 s**
- line 3: 8.00 s in a 7 s window, **+1.00 s**
- line 7: 3.44 s in a 3 s window, **+0.44 s**
- line 8: 10.00 s in a 9 s window, **+1.00 s**

The edit absorbs them rather than speeding the voice: line 2 starts at 8.00
and runs into the glance, line 3 starts at 14.60, line 4 at 22.60, and the
close-half still starts at 25.00 (the first command is spoken at 25.90).
Line 5 fits 25–41. Lines 7 and 8 start on their spoken cues (46.40 and
49.90) so the last line ends at 59.90 and the cut stays 60.0 s.

Regenerate the voice and the timing file with:

```bash
python3 video/scripts/generate-vo-v4.py \
  --lines video/vo/lines-v4.json \
  --env-file /Users/user/Code/kota-kita/tools/tts/.env
python3 video/scripts/build-vo-v4.py
```

`generate-vo-v4.py` writes the MP3s and word alignments, trims no speech, and
normalises each clip to about −16 LUFS. `build-vo-v4.py` writes
`src/voV4.generated.ts`: `VO_CLIPS`, the word-synced `CAPTIONS_1MIN` chunks
(2–7 words), and the `CUES` that drive the close-half HUD states. The key is
read from the Kota Kita env file and never printed or committed.

The captions show the exact spoken text in 2–7 word chunks, each from its
first word's start to its last word's end; the current word is highlighted.
They are not paraphrases and do not appear outside their audio.

### Mix

The bed sits at **25%** (`MUSIC_LEVEL = 0.25`) and ducks to **9%**
(`MUSIC_DUCKED = 0.09`) under each VO clip with an 8-frame ramp
(`musicVolume` in `src/OneMinute.tsx`). The v4 VO clips are normalised to
about −16 LUFS; the integrated mix of the review render is about −16.9 LUFS
with a −4.0 dBFS true peak.

The 60 s cut must be rendered with both audio props:

## Repo link

`src/config.ts` already defaults to `github.com/artomily/wicksight`. To render a
different link without editing the file:

```bash
npx remotion render src/index.ts WickSight out/wicksight.mp4 \
  --props='{"repo":"github.com/<owner>/wicksight","music":false}'
```

Props are validated against `defaultProps` in `src/config.ts`.

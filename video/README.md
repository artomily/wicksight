# WickSight — demo videos (Remotion)

Two cuts, both 1920×1080, 30 fps, silent, with captions:

- **60 s key-features cut** (`WickSight1Min`) — the submitted video. The release
  asset `wicksight-final.mp4` is this cut: cold 0–5 s, glance 5–15 s, voice
  alert 15–25 s, close half 25–42 s, receipt 42–50 s, safety/logo/links 50–60 s.
- **2:59 full cut** (`WickSight`) — cold open + hook + reveal + seven proof
  beats + safety + CTA. The release asset `wicksight-full.mp4` is the original
  2:55 full cut; `wicksight-final-v2.mp4` is the 2:59 cold-open full cut.

Every frame is generated by Remotion; there is no screen recording and nothing
is captured live. The cold open and proof beats are labelled replays of the real
Binance Futures **testnet** HUD from 23 Nov 2026, rebuilt from the captures in
`public/`.

## Honesty rules

- The cold open is **not live footage** either. It is the same 23 Nov testnet
  reconstruction, labelled `REPLAY · RECORDED 23 NOV 2026 · BINANCE FUTURES
  TESTNET` and `REPLAY · NO LIVE MARKET DATA` on screen.
- The proof section is **not live footage**. It is a reconstruction from the
  23 Nov 2026 testnet captures, labelled `REPLAY`, `TESTNET PROOF · REPLAY` and
  `NO LIVE MARKET DATA` on screen.
- The captured values are used throughout: entry ≈ 61,492.9, mark 61,486.88,
  liq 58,684.4, distance 4.6%, position 0.01 BTCUSDT long at 20×, half-fill at
  61,502.9, 0.005 left.
- The voice beat uses `alert 61,500` so the armed alert matches the captured
  alert hit at 61,486.88. The app's listening screen still shows its generic
  `alert 62,000` prompt; the command itself is a different price. The parser
  supports any price.
- The ETA uses a clean 700 USDT/min tape toward the captured liq line, so the
  HUD reads `LIQ IN ~4m at this pace · buffer 4.6%`. The live app fits the last
  three minutes of real 1 s ticks and hides the line when the tape is flat,
  noisy or moving away.
- The receipt uses the recorded pre-close position and the exchange's
  isolated-margin formula to derive the post-close liq (`58,684 → 55,591`,
  buffer `4.6% → 9.6%`). The live app takes both values from the Binance
  position snapshots before and after the order, not from this formula.
- The reveal shows the captured −0.06 USDT exactly; the proof HUD ticks around
  it. Both are replays, not a live feed.
- `v1.0.0` replaces the old `wicksight-final.mp4` asset with this cut so the
  already-submitted link keeps working; the local render is
  `wicksight-final-v2.mp4`.

## Music and voiceover

Neither is bundled (no copyrighted music). To add them:

- **Music:** put a licensed track at `video/public/music.mp3`, then render with
  `--props='{"music":true}'`. Volume is set to 0.32 in `src/WickSight.tsx`.
- **Voiceover:** put `video/public/vo.mp3` (2:59), render with
  `--props='{"voiceover":true}'`. Captions follow the script in
  `docs/demo-script.md`; the video's voice beat uses `alert 61,500`, so update
  the VO or the caption if you record a different number.

## Repo link

`src/config.ts` already defaults to `github.com/artomily/wicksight`. To render a
different link without editing the file:

```bash
npx remotion render src/index.ts WickSight out/wicksight.mp4 \
  --props='{"repo":"github.com/<owner>/wicksight","music":false}'
```

Props are validated against `defaultProps` in `src/config.ts`.
